# Idea Selection

We scored all 50 brainstorm ideas (Units 1, 4, 5, 6, 8) on **difficulty**, **how well it would land on LinkedIn**, and **hours to build**. Full ranking was done in chat on Nov 26.

## Pick: Unit 6, Tier 3 #10 — "What would AI data centers have to earn?"
| | Score |
|---|---|
| Difficulty | 2/5 (easiest in Tier 3) |
| LinkedIn appeal | 5/5 (best in Tier 3) |

**Why:** the single biggest capital budgeting story in the economy right now, it needs only a handful of numbers, and the verification work *is* the "where AI got it wrong" section (see [[07 AI Error Log]]).

**Runner-ups (if we pivot):** Unit 8 #10 *Do active managers earn alpha?* (5/5 appeal, 10–12 hrs); Unit 5 #8 *Dow vs. S&P 500* (4/5 appeal, 5–7 hrs).

## Original brainstorm text
> **What would AI data centers have to earn?**
> Concept: Case question 1 applied to the largest capital spending program in the economy.
> Build: Start with the roughly $725 billion that Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, and Microsoft plan to spend in 2026. Choose a required return and a useful life (servers wear out in about five or six years; buildings last decades), and compute the annual cash flow needed for an NPV of zero. Compare it with the cloud and AI revenue those companies report today.
> Data: Company earnings releases; 10-K and 10-Q segment revenue (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Intelligent Cloud) and depreciation useful-life notes.
